Echo: Time Loop Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Echo: Time Loop Puzzle ("Echo", "the app") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Echo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only in local storage on your own device:

  • solved levels, stars and best results;
  • daily puzzle streak;
  • settings (sound, vibration, language) and which tutorial tips you have seen.

This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

Echo may use vibration (haptic feedback), which you can turn off in Settings. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it works without an internet connection.

Children

Echo is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Echo: Vaqt halqasi jumbog‘i - Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 28-sentabr

Echo: Vaqt halqasi jumbog‘i ("Echo", "ilova") - internetsiz ishlaydigan jumboq o‘yini. Ushbu siyosat ilovadan foydalanganingizda ma’lumotlaringiz bilan nima sodir bo‘lishini tushuntiradi.

Qanday ma’lumot yig‘iladi

Hech qanday. Echo hech qanday shaxsiy yoki foydalanish ma’lumotini yig‘maydi, serverga yubormaydi, boshqalarga bermaydi va sotmaydi. Ilovada akkaunt, analitika, reklama SDK’lari va hech qanday kuzatuv yo‘q.

Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma’lumotlar

Natijalaringizni eslab qolish uchun ilova quyidagilarni faqat o‘z qurilmangizdagi mahalliy xotirada saqlaydi:

  • yechilgan bosqichlar, yulduzlar va eng yaxshi natijalar;
  • kunlik jumboq ketma-ketligi;
  • sozlamalar (ovoz, tebranish, til) va ko‘rilgan maslahatlar.

Bu ma’lumotlar qurilmangizdan tashqariga chiqmaydi. Ularni istalgan vaqtda Sozlamalar → Natijalarni o‘chirish orqali, ilova xotirasini tozalab yoki ilovani o‘chirib yuborib yo‘q qilishingiz mumkin.

Ruxsatlar

Echo tebranishdan (haptik javob) foydalanishi mumkin, uni Sozlamalarda o‘chirib qo‘yish mumkin. Ilova joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, rasmlar yoki fayllarga kirmaydi va internetsiz ishlaydi.

Bolalar

Echo barcha yoshdagilar uchun mos. Hech qanday ma’lumot yig‘ilmagani sababli bolalarning shaxsiy ma’lumotlari ham yig‘ilmaydi.

O‘zgarishlar

Agar siyosat o‘zgarsa, yangilangan matn shu manzilda yangi sana bilan e’lon qilinadi.
